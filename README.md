## App data handling

Toe Tac Tic is offline-first.

The app stores gameplay progress and audio preferences locally on the device.

Game Center is optional and is only used when the player chooses online ranking or live matchmaking.

The app does not include ads, third-party analytics, or tracking.

## App Store submission notes

Use the support and privacy documents in this repository as the basis for your App Store Connect Support URL and Privacy Policy URL content.

The iOS target also includes a `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` manifest so the app bundle declares no tracking and no app-defined collected data.
