# Toe Tac Tic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-24

## Summary

Toe Tac Tic is designed to work offline by default.

The app does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies.

## Data stored on the device

Toe Tac Tic stores the following data locally on the device using on-device web storage:

- Coins earned in the game
- Unlocked difficulty levels
- Total score
- Music and sound effect preferences

This local data is used only to restore your progress and settings on the same device.

## Optional Game Center features

If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, the app can use Game Center for:

- Leaderboards
- Ranked score display
- Live matchmaking and multiplayer matches

When you use these features, the app may access Game Center account information made available by Apple, such as your Game Center alias, display name, player identifier, rank, and score.

Toe Tac Tic uses that information only to:

- Show your Game Center identity inside the app
- Load leaderboard entries
- Submit your score
- Create and manage live matches

Game Center services are provided by Apple and are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

## Data sharing

Toe Tac Tic does not sell your data.

Toe Tac Tic does not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Outside of Apple's optional Game Center services that you explicitly use, the app does not send personal data to third parties.

## Tracking

Toe Tac Tic does not track users across apps or websites.

The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

## Your choices

You can use Toe Tac Tic offline without signing in to Game Center.

You can remove the app's locally stored progress at any time from the in-app Privacy and support section using the Reset local progress action.

Removing the app from your device also removes locally stored app data from that device. Game Center records managed by Apple may remain available in your Game Center account.
